The Alliance Against Food Waste is a coalition of people who have looked at a half-eaten plate and said: not on my watch. Our primary objective is simple: we eat up. Our secondary objective is the university fruit basket, which we rescue, according to a strict and legally dubious protocol, before a single banana goes brown.

Founded in October 2026 by Luka Bekavac and Simon Mayer at the Future Society Hub (FuSo) of the University of St.Gallen (HSG) in Switzerland, the alliance asks its members to finish what is on their plate, take only what they will eat, and take the university fruit basket home before it spoils.

An initiative of Future Society Hub where tech and society fuse (and lunch gets finished) futuresociety.ch ↗

Food is for eating. So we eat it.

Roughly a third of all food produced for people is lost or wasted, according to the UN's Food and Agriculture Organization. We cannot fix global supply chains over lunch, but we can fix the last thirty centimetres between the plate and the bin.

Membership is open to anyone with a mouth and a sense of responsibility. There are no fees, only seconds.

The Fruit Basket Protocol

Our university has a fruit basket. It appears, it is admired, and then, too often, it quietly turns into compost. Not any more. The alliance operates under the following policy, reproduced here in full and without amendment.

If there is fruit left on the first Friday after the fruit basket has appeared (that’s today), start taking fruit home. If there is fruit left on the second Friday after the fruit basket has appeared, you must take fruit home. If you see a fruit basket on your way in, take it and put it on the table. It is permitted to arrange fruit in ways that afford taking/eating.

The Policy, as announced on Friday, 30 October 2026

Day 0

Fruit is for eating at work. Grab an apple. Grab two. Nobody is counting, except the alliance.

First Friday after

Fruit left? Start taking it home.

The weekend is coming and the fruit will not survive it alone. Taking fruit home is now encouraged.

Not a suggestion. This is a rescue mission, and you are the helicopter.

§1

First Friday: permission

If there is fruit left on the first Friday after the basket has appeared, members start taking fruit home.

Commentary“Start” is deliberately open-ended. One pear is a start. So is a bag.

§2

Second Friday: obligation

If there is fruit left on the second Friday after the basket has appeared, members must take fruit home.

Commentary“I don't like kiwis” is not a recognised defence. Make a smoothie.

§3

The delivery clause

If you see a fruit basket on your way in, take it and put it on the table.

CommentaryA basket in the hallway is a basket nobody eats from. You are its last mile.

§4

The affordance clause

It is permitted to arrange fruit in ways that afford taking/eating.

CommentaryInteraction design applies to bananas too. Ripest on top, the lonely last apple front and centre, nothing buried under the melon.

Frequently asked questions

Answered by the founding members, with their mouths full.

What is the Alliance Against Food Waste?

A light-hearted initiative of the Future Society Hub at the University of St.Gallen, Switzerland, against food waste. Members pledge to eat up: finish what is on their plate, take only what they will finish, and follow the Fruit Basket Protocol so that the university's fruit is eaten rather than thrown away.

Who founded the alliance?

Luka Bekavac and Simon Mayer, both of the Future Society Hub (FuSo) at the University of St.Gallen, founded it in October 2026.

What is the Fruit Basket Protocol?

The alliance's rule for the university fruit basket. If fruit is left on the first Friday after the basket appeared, members start taking fruit home; if fruit is left on the second Friday, they must take it home. Anyone who sees a fruit basket on their way in carries it to the table, and arranging the fruit so it invites eating is explicitly allowed.

How do I join?

Sign the pledge at the bottom of this page to get your membership card. To appear on the list of members, submit the pre-filled GitHub issue that the card links to; a founding member approves it and your name is added automatically.

What if I am genuinely full?

Then you took too much, and Principle 02 has a word for you. In the meantime: box it, take it home, eat it later. Full is temporary. Leftovers keep.

Does a banana peel count as food waste?

No. Peels, cores, pits and stones are exempt. The alliance is ambitious, not unhinged. (Compost them, though.)

The basket appeared on a Friday. Is that the first Friday?

No. The first Friday is the first one after the basket appeared, so you get a full week of eating at work first. The Basket-o-meter knows this.

Somebody else left food on their plate. What do I do?

Lead by example. Finish your own plate, visibly and with relish. Direct intervention in other people's lunches is reserved for founding members, and only if they ask nicely.

Can I leave the alliance?

Yes. But the fruit will know.

Is there a membership fee?

No. Membership is paid for entirely in clean plates.
